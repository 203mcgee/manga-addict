About Us

Manga Addict! is a e-commerece that sells manga and other comics. We have loved manga and wanted other people to get the chance to understand this medium. Therefore, we started this website to be able to provide manga or comic readers access to their favorite series. Also, we have many genre of manga like shounen,shojo,seinen,josei, and many more. We will be able to provide consumers with online version of manga and we could also ship physical them to their home.
